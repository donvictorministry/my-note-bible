Privacy Policy

DV Note & Bible. Effective October 1, 2026.

In short

DV Note & Bible has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your notes, their titles and links.
  • Any 4-digit PIN you set. It is stored only as a one-way scrambled value, never as the number itself.
  • Your settings, text size, to-do list, saved Bible text and daily verse streak.

All of this is kept in the app's storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it.

When the app uses the internet

Only when you tap Download Bible. The app then asks the getBible service (api.getbible.net) for the King James Bible text. Like any website, getBible may see your IP address and the time of the request, under its own policy. After the download, the Bible works without internet.

Sharing

When you choose Share or Copy link on a note, the note is placed inside a link and handed to the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or SMS. Anyone who has that link can open the note. A locked note is encrypted with its PIN inside the link. You decide who receives a link.

Other apps and websites

The contact buttons on the Verse page open WhatsApp, Facebook, your email app or your phone app when you tap them. Those apps follow their own privacy policies.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
